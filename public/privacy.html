Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Virtual Hookah is a camera toy that runs in your browser. Just for fun, no smoking involved. This page explains what data it uses.

Your camera stays on your device

  • Face, hand and background tracking run entirely in your browser. Camera images, video and face or hand landmarks are never uploaded or stored by us.
  • In online video calls, your video and audio go directly between the players' devices (peer-to-peer). They may pass through a relay server only to get through restrictive networks, encrypted, and are never recorded or stored.

What we store when you create an account

  • Account: your email address and a sign-in method (password, stored hashed, or Google sign-in). With Google we receive your email address and Google account ID; we don't use your contacts, files or anything else.
  • Profile: your username, avatar emoji, and when you were last online (shown to friends).
  • Friends: friend requests, friendships and blocks.
  • Game data: scores of runs you save (score, rings, longest hold, number of hits, time), and the online rooms you create or join.
  • Reports: if you report a player, your report and its reason.

Without an account you can still play; your settings and local stats are kept only in your browser's local storage.

Who can see it

  • Everyone (including visitors) can see the leaderboard: usernames, avatars and best scores.
  • Signed-in players can find you by username. Friends can see when you were last online.
  • Your email address is never shown to other players.

Where it is stored

Account and game data are stored with our backend provider Supabase (data centre in Singapore). The website is served by our hosting provider. We do not sell your data, show ads or use third-party trackers.

Your choices

  • Change your username or avatar at any time (username once a week).
  • Delete your account in Profile → Delete account: this permanently deletes your account, profile, friends and scores.
  • Block or report players who bother you.

Security

Access to data is enforced by the database itself (row-level security): you can only read and change what you are allowed to. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

Children

Virtual Hookah is a toy, not a tobacco product, and does not promote smoking. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: open an issue on the project's GitHub page or use the in-app report feature.
