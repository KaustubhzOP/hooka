Virtual Hookah

Hold a real pen like a hookah mouthpiece. Your lips and hand drive a virtual inhale → hold → exhale, with smoke.

  1. Bring the pen to your lips and purse them, like sipping through it, to inhale.
  2. Relax your lips (or pull the pen away) to hold it in.
  3. Purse your lips again to blow the smoke out. Hold too long and you'll cough.

🔒 Tracking runs entirely in your browser. No video or images leave your device.

Tips

  • Run Calibrate (C) once: it learns how pursed your lips look.
  • Face the camera with good, even light. Keep your hand in view.
  • Open the mouth wide while holding and the smoke leaks out.
  • Rings: while holding, make an “O” with your mouth (lips rounded, jaw open). Close, then “O” again for the next ring.
  • Nose smoke: while holding, scrunch your nose. Some smoke also leaves your nose on every exhale (slider above).
  • Keys: 1–5 smoke · O test ring · N test nose · R reset · L landmarks
