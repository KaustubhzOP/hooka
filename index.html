Virtual Hookah

Hold a real pen like a hookah mouthpiece. Your lips and hand drive a virtual inhale → hold → exhale, with smoke, rings and tricks.

  1. Pen to your lips and sip through it to inhale.
  2. Relax your lips to hold the smoke.
  3. Purse to blow, make an “O” for rings, or scrunch your nose for dragon smoke.

🔒 Tracking runs entirely in your browser. No video or images leave your device.

Achievements

  • Calibrate first (C): 4 short steps (relax, sip with the pen, “O”, scrunch nose). It learns your face, so every gesture is recognised reliably.
  • Inhale: pen at your lips, sip. Hold: relax your lips.
  • Blow: purse your lips (mouth nearly closed).
  • Rings: make an “O” (lips round, jaw open). Close, then “O” again for the next ring. Each ring uses some smoke.
  • Dragon: scrunch your nose to exhale through it. A little smoke also leaves your nose on every exhale.
  • French inhale: while holding, tilt your head back with your mouth slightly open.
  • Ghost: open your mouth wide: a ball of smoke rolls out. Sip quickly to pull it back in!
  • Waterfall: tilt your head down while blowing.
  • Bend the smoke: wave your hand through it, or blow at it after an exhale.
  • Hold too long and you cough. Watch the chips at the top: they show what the camera sees.
  • Good, even light on your face helps a lot. Keep the hand with the pen in view.
